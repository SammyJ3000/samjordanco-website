Independent software development with the judgement of a designer. Clear enough for a business owner, detailed enough to reward a closer look. This guide records the second browser concept and the selected near-black, soft white and lilac palette; the final identity is still open to review.

The palette

Near-black gives the work a dramatic ground. Soft white keeps long passages calm. Lilac identifies the next useful action and connects the interface details.

Space to
understand.

Open layouts establish hierarchy. The work gets generous space; the controls sit close to what they change. Rounded corners belong to interactive surfaces, not every paragraph.

  • Desktop containerUp to 85rem, with fluid page gutters.
  • Editorial rhythmLarge section breaks, compact groups of related content.
  • Shapes16–20px image and panel corners; pill-shaped controls.
  • DepthA layered project deck. Quiet, flat reading surfaces.

Motion you
can handle.

The signature interaction is real work you can pick up and browse. Motion follows input, carries momentum, and accepts a new direction before it finishes.

  • Project deckDirect drag, projected release and an interruptible spring.
  • Scroll sequenceThe app, design system, brand and journey rise vertically over one another. Each card adapts to the available screen height. Software case studies use smaller, complete previews in ordinary page flow.
  • Motion controlA visible pause control stops background motion and removes scroll-linked travel.
  • View changesA short transition preserves context as the content changes.
  • Keyboard and reduced motionImmediate selection, visible focus and no movement required.
  • PurposeFeedback, orientation and continuity. Every effect has a job.
