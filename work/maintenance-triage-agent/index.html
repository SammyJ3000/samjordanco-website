Property Maintenance Triage

Tenant reports a leak, the agent triages it, prices it, picks a contractor and puts one decision in front of the landlord.

AI tool Property 2026 prototype

The problem

A tenant messages "there is water coming through the kitchen ceiling" at nine on a Sunday night. What follows is a chain of small delays: someone reads it Monday, asks the tenant three follow-up questions, works out whether it is urgent, finds a plumber, gets a rough price, and asks the landlord to approve.

None of those steps is hard. All of them are waiting. And the cost of the delay is not admin time — it is the ceiling.

What I built

An agent that takes the tenant message in whatever form it arrives, classifies the trade and urgency, produces a plain-English summary, estimates a rough price band from the trade and risk language, and recommends a contractor and a dispatch mode.

The landlord sees one card with one decision on it. There is an approval rule with a quote limit, so anything urgent under the limit can be auto-accepted and anything above it waits for a human. The point is not to remove the landlord, it is to remove the four messages that used to happen before the landlord could decide anything.

Requests move across a status board — open, urgent, ready, waiting on tenant info — so the thing that is stuck is visible rather than sitting in somebody's inbox.
