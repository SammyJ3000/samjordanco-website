World Cup Sweepstake App

A 100+ player sweepstake that would have been a spreadsheet nightmare, run as a live app instead.

The problem

A private sweepstake with over a hundred players is fine right up until the first set of results comes in.

Then it becomes one person manually matching entries to teams, recalculating points, rewriting a league table, and answering the same three questions in a group chat for a month.

What I built

A web app that handles entries, team allocation, match results, points, rankings and live standings.

Results go in once. Everything downstream — every player total, the full table, each person's own position — recalculates from that single input. The organiser stopped being the bottleneck, and players stopped asking, because they could just look.

What changed

The tournament ran without a single manual table rebuild. The interesting part was that engagement went up: when standings are live, people check them.
