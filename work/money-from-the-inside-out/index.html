Money From the Inside Out

A financial wellbeing app, with a world of its own.

Mobile app Financial wellbeing 2026 Front end complete · Backend planned

The starting point

My client came to me with a prototype she had built using Claude Code and a clear idea for the app she wanted to bring to the App Store.

My role was to take that starting point into a working React Native application, with the screens, interactions and visual identity developed together.

What I built

My role: Product design, visual identity and React Native front-end development

I designed and built the React Native front end, including integrations, animations and games. The illustrated world gives the app its own setting and connects the experience across its screens.

Alongside the application, I created the brand kit, colour system and graphics. Product design, interface details and visual identity were developed as parts of the same product.

The implementation carries that system into the working app, including the motion and interaction details that static designs cannot show. The selected screens here have been approved by the client for my portfolio.
