You don’t need a technical brief. You need a clear way from what you have to what you want to achieve.

An app or a prototypeIt looks promising. Making it work is the hard part.

A product people can actually use.

A prototype can show the idea without solving the details: how the screens connect, how it feels to use, or how it fits together as a product.

I bring the interface, brand, interactions and development together, then connect the services it needs. We agree the backend and release work up front, so you have a coherent product and a clear next step.

A better way of workingThe work is moving. Keeping track of it is another job.

Less chasing. A clearer view of the work.

Jobs sit in spreadsheets. Updates arrive in messages. Someone has to piece it all together before the team can see what needs doing next.

I map how your team works and build a shared place for the jobs, information and decisions that matter. Scheduling, tracking and communication fit your process, so people can spend less time chasing updates and more time getting the work done.

An AI integrationThe demo is impressive. The useful part is less clear.

AI with a useful job to do.

You can see the potential, but a separate chat window is not always the answer. The real challenge is connecting AI to the information and workflow your business already uses.

We start with a specific task, then I build the connections, interface and review controls around it. Your team gets a feature they can use in context, with clear ways to check the output and stay in control.
